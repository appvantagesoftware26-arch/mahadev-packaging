Request Account Deletion

What May Be Deleted

Depending on the application and applicable requirements, account deletion may include deletion or anonymization of personal information such as:

  • Account profile information.
  • Name.
  • Phone number.
  • Email address.
  • Authentication-related information.
  • Personal preferences.
  • Other personal information associated with the account.

Information That May Need to Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, accounting requirements, legitimate business requirements, fraud prevention, security requirements, dispute resolution, or other applicable obligations.

Where information must be retained, it will be retained only for the period reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

Business and Administrative Accounts

Administrative accounts may have access to customer, order, product, billing, inventory, or other business information.

Deletion of an administrative account may therefore require verification or approval from the relevant client business or authorized account owner.

Deleting an administrator's account does not necessarily mean that the business records created or managed through that account will be deleted.

Client Applications

If the application is operated on behalf of a particular business, the relevant business may be involved in verifying and processing account deletion requests.

Processing Requests

We will review deletion requests and take appropriate action within a reasonable period, subject to identity verification, technical requirements, legal obligations, and applicable business-record retention requirements.
