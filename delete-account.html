Delete Account Request

Application: Mahadev Packaging

If you no longer wish to use the Mahadev Packaging application, you may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request account deletion, please send an email to:

Email: support@appvantage.in

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your registered mobile number
  • Your name (optional but recommended)
  • The subject line: Account Deletion Request

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon verification of your request, we will delete the following information associated with your account where applicable:

  • Your account information
  • Your profile details
  • Your saved delivery addresses
  • Your login information associated with the application

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required by applicable law, taxation, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate business or legal purposes.

Examples may include completed order records, invoices, or transaction information that we are legally required to retain.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 7 business days. If additional verification is required, processing may take longer.
