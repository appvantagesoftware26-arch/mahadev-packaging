Privacy Policy

Mahadev Packaging

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Mahadev Packaging ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information of people who use our applications and services.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mahadev Packaging Shopping Application ("Shopping App") and the Mahadev Packaging Administration Application ("Admin App").

Because the two applications provide different functionality, the type of information accessed or processed may differ depending on which application you use and which features you access.

Important: The Shopping App and Admin App do not necessarily access the same information. For example, the Shopping App may use location information for relevant shopping or delivery functionality, while the Admin App does not use device location. The Admin App may allow authorized administrators to upload product images.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

Depending on the application and authentication method, we may process information such as:

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address, where applicable
  • Login and authentication information
  • Account status and account-related information

We use this information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, provide application functionality, and protect the security of the service.

1.2 Shopping and Order Information

When using the Shopping App, we may process information necessary to provide shopping and order functionality, including:

  • Products viewed or selected
  • Cart information
  • Orders
  • Order items
  • Delivery information
  • Billing-related information
  • Order status and transaction-related records

1.3 Location Information

The Shopping App may request access to device location when location is necessary for a feature such as delivery, address-related functionality, or other location-based services.

Location access is not required for the Admin App and the Admin App does not intentionally collect device location information.

Location information is used only for the relevant application functionality and is not intended to be used for unrelated purposes.

1.4 Product Images and Uploaded Images

The Admin App may allow authorized administrators to upload images when creating or editing product listings.

These images may be uploaded to our server or storage infrastructure and associated with product records so that the images can be displayed as part of the product catalog.

The Shopping App does not require administrators' product-image upload functionality.

1.5 Customer Information Available to Administrators

Authorized administrators using the Admin App may be able to access information required to manage customer orders, generate bills, manage products, and perform other administrative functions.

Access to this information is restricted to authorized users and is intended for legitimate business and application-management purposes.

1.6 Device and Technical Information

Depending on how the applications are configured and used, technical information may be processed, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system information
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Technical logs
  • Error and diagnostic information

Such information may be used to maintain application security, troubleshoot problems, improve reliability, and prevent abuse.

  • Manage product listings.
